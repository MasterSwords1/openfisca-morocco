## Q1. Was Décret 2-23-1067 amended after Décret 2-24-706? Full amending chain with BO number and date

### Takeaway
Yes — Décret 2-23-1067 has been amended **twice** since 2-24-706: once on 23 July 2026 (Décret 2-26-434, adding Art. 10 bis on the duration of the new exceptional allowance) and, on 12 November 2026, by a **Décret 2-26-838 that was only approved by the Conseil de gouvernement and, as of 16 November 2026, has no identified BO publication**. Décret 2-26-838 is the only text that changes the 500 DH minimum, and it changes it **from 1 January 2027, not in 2026**.

### Cited Findings
- **Décret n° 2.23.1067 of 17 Jumada I 1445 (1 December 2023)**, implementing Loi 58-23 — original text, listed by ANSS with the Arabic PDF — [ANSS, Lois & décrets](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/lois-decrets). The law it implements was promulgated by dahir 1.23.87 of 30 November 2023 and published in **BO n° 7253 of 4 December 2023, p. 10223** — [maroclaw (footnote citing BO 7253)](https://maroclaw.com/%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%B1-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%BA%D8%B1%D8%A8/).
- **Décret n° 2.24.706 of 19 Muharram 1446 (25 July 2024)**, "complétant l'annexe du décret n° 2.23.1067", **BO n° 7321 of 29 July 2024** — [ANSS, Lois & décrets](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/lois-decrets). Content: it adds the **school-return allowance** to the annex — 200 DH per child in primary/lower-secondary, 300 DH per child in upper-secondary (qualifiant), capped at 6 children per household, paid once a year in September, reserved to public-sector pupils, in the framework of the "Un million de cartables" programme — [Le Matin, 24 July 2024](https://lematin.ma/nation/rentree-scolaire-des-aides-directes-aux-familles-des-septembre/236534).
- **Décret n° 2.25.1064 of 2 Rajab 1447 (23 December 2025)**, "relatif au bénéfice de l'aide spéciale", **BO n° 7468 of 25 December 2025** — [ANSS, Lois & décrets](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/lois-decrets). This is **not** an amendment of 2.23.1067; it implements Art. 16 of Loi 58-23 (orphan/abandoned children in care institutions). Its Art. 11 sets the special allowance at **500 DH/month**; Art. 12 requires the money to be deposited in a CDG account in the child's name; Art. 13 sets a **floor of 10,000 DH** payable to the child at majority; Art. 15 says it enters into force **three months after publication** (≈ 25 March 2026) — [BO 7468 PDF, pp. 9966–9967](https://anss.gov.ma/sites/default/files/anss/de%CC%81cret%202-25-1064%20aide%20spe%CC%81ciale.pdf). **[VERIFIED — read in the Arabic text]**
- **Décret n° 2-26-434 of 8 Safar 1448 (23 July 2026)**, "complétant le décret n° 2-23-1067", **BO n° 7530 of 30 July 2026** (p. 4852). It inserts **Article 10 bis**: «تحدد مدة الاستفادة من المنحة الاستثنائية، المنصوص عليها في المادة 16 المكررة من القانون السالف الذكر رقم 58.23، في اثني عشر (12) شهرا، بصفة متصلة أو غير متصلة» ("the duration of the exceptional allowance provided for in Article 16 bis of Law 58-23 is set at twelve (12) months, continuous or not") — [BO 7530 PDF, p. 4852](https://anss.gov.ma/sites/default/files/anss/pages/%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%85%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%AD%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AB%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%8A%D9%94%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf). **[VERIFIED — read in the Arabic text]**
- **Décret n° 2.26.838**, "modifiant le décret n° 2.23.1067", **approved by the Conseil de gouvernement on Thursday 12 November 2026**: it raises the flat/minimum allowance **from 500 to 600 DH per month with effect from 1 January 2027**; further increases are programmed to reach 1,000 DH by 2031 — [Hespress FR, 12 Nov 2026](https://fr.hespress.com/496205-conseil-de-gouvernement-adoption-dun-projet-de-decret-relatif-au-regime-des-aides-sociales-directes.html); [Médias24, 12 Nov 2026](https://medias24.com/2026/11/12/aide-sociale-directe-le-gouvernement-acte-par-decret-la-hausse-du-minimum-a-600-dh-des-janvier-2027-1790384/); [maroc.ma](https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/conseil-du-gouvernement-adoption-dun-projet-de-decret-relatif-au-regime-des-aides-sociales-directes). **[VERIFIED that it was approved as a "projet de décret"; NOT VERIFIED that it has been published in the BO]**
- Médias24 adds the fiscal context: the PLF 2027 costs the first 100 DH step at **3 billion DH**; and at end-September 2026 the scheme covered **just over 4 million households and ~5.8 million children**, with 71 billion DH paid since December 2023; **~39% of beneficiary households receive the flat allowance** (no children or no child-related entitlement) — [Médias24, 12 Nov 2026](https://medias24.com/2026/11/12/aide-sociale-directe-le-gouvernement-acte-par-decret-la-hausse-du-minimum-a-600-dh-des-janvier-2027-1790384/).

**Consolidated amount schedule as published by ANSS (accessed 16 Nov 2026)** — [ANSS, Typologies des aides](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/typologies-d-aides) **[VERIFIED as ANSS's official rendering]**:

|---|---|---|---|

Plus: **birth grant 2,000 DH (1st birth) / 1,000 DH (2nd birth)**, unchanged, not multiplied for multiple births; **school-return 200/300 DH per child** (Sept., max 6 children); **flat allowance 500 DH/month** for households aged 21+ without dependent children.

### Inferences
- The **child amount schedule was never rewritten by an amending decree after 2023**; the 2025 and 2026 figures are staged columns of the original annex (see Q3). The only post-2024 decree that touches 2.23.1067's *substance* is 2-26-434 (procedure/duration, no amounts) and the still-unpublished 2-26-838 (minimum only).
- The **500 → 600 DH step must not be modelled as effective in 2026**. Any 2026 parameterisation should keep the floor at 500.

### Gaps
- No BO publication reference exists yet for **2.26.838** in any source consulted (searches for "2.26.838" return only 12 Nov 2026 government-council coverage). Its BO number and date, and whether it also touches child tiers, are **NOT CHECKED**. A minority-signal Le Matin social-media snippet spoke of "la première augmentation des montants de l'aide sociale directe **pour les enfants**" and of "250 dirhams par enfant scolarisé", but every other source (Hespress, Médias24, maroc.ma, MAP) describes only a **minimum** increase, and 250 DH was already the 2025 figure — I treat the snippet as garbled/dated and **do not** report a 2027 child-tier increase as fact.
- Whether Décret 2.24.706 did anything beyond adding the school-return table (one Arabic-language secondary source says it also "précise la notion de chef de famille") could not be verified from the primary text — [juritheque](https://juritheque.com/loi/decret-2-23-1067-application-soutien-social-direct).

## Q2. Were Loi 58-23 articles 1–4 and 7–12 amended?

### Takeaway
**No.** The only amendment to Loi 58-23 in force is **Loi n° 41.26** (dahir 1.26.23 of 25 June 2026, BO 7523 of 6 July 2026), and it operates exclusively by **inserting Articles 16 bis and 16 bis bis** and **rewriting Article 17**. Articles 1–4 and 7–12 are untouched.

### Cited Findings
- **Loi n° 41.26 modifiant et complétant la loi n° 58.23**, promulgated by **dahir n° 1-26-23 of 9 Muharram 1448 (25 June 2026)**, published **BO n° 7523 of 6 July 2026** — [ANSS, Lois & décrets](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/lois-decrets) and [BO 7523 PDF, pp. 4200–4201](https://anss.gov.ma/sites/default/files/anss/pages/loi%20041.26.pdf); BO reference corroborated by [juritheque BO index 7523](https://juritheque.com/bo/7523). **[VERIFIED — read in the Arabic text]**
- Structure of Loi 41.26, verbatim from the BO:
  - **Article premier**: «يتمم القانون رقم 58.23 ... بالمادتين 16 المكررة و16 المكررة مرتين التاليتين» — inserts **16 bis** and **16 bis bis**.
  - **Article 2**: «تغير، على النحو التالي، أحكام المادة 17» — amends **Article 17** only.
  - **Article 3**: «تدخل أحكام هذا القانون حيز التنفيذ ابتداء من تاريخ نشر النص التنظيمي اللازم لتطبيقه بالجريدة الرسمية» — entry into force **on publication of the implementing regulation** (that regulation is Décret 2-26-434, BO 7530 of 30 July 2026). **[VERIFIED]**
- Operative provision inserted as **Article 16 bis** (quoted from the BO): «تخول منحة استثنائية لكل أسرة كانت تستفيد من الإعانات الممنوحة في إطار نظام الدعم الاجتماعي المباشر وفقدت الحق في هذه الاستفادة بسبب التصريح بأحد الزوجين أو رب الأسرة بنظام الضمان الاجتماعي الجاري به العمل في القطاع الخاص» — an **exceptional allowance** for any household that was receiving ASD and **lost entitlement because the head of household or a spouse was declared to the private-sector social-security scheme (CNSS)**. It is granted **once**, for a duration fixed by regulation (12 months, per Décret 2-26-434), its **amount equals the allowance(s) the household would have received**, and Articles 9, 10, 11 and Title II of the law apply to it. **[VERIFIED]**
- **Article 16 bis bis**: if the spouse/head of household **loses their job**, the household may again receive ASD allowances **without being bound by the minimum period in Article 7(2)** (the CNSS-declaration look-back window), but may not re-claim the exceptional allowance except within the limit set by Article 16 bis ¶2. **[VERIFIED]**
- **Article 17 as amended** now reads: «تتولى تدبير .............. المباشر والمنحة الاستثنائية المنصوص عليها في المادة 16 المكررة أعلاه، هيئة تحدث لهذا الغرض» — management of the scheme and of the exceptional allowance is entrusted to "a body created for that purpose" (previously the ANSS by name). **[VERIFIED]**
- The pre-existing architecture therefore still governs: Art. 1 (three components: childhood-risk allowances, flat allowance, special allowance for institutionalised orphans/abandoned children); Art. 2 (monthly allowance per child **under 21, max 6 children**, amount by age and schooling + **complementary support** for disability and for **paternal orphans up to 3 children**, and the **birth grant for the 1st and 2nd births**); Art. 3 (conditions: child under 21; birth-grant claim within 6 months of birth; residence in Morocco; registration in the RSU; **meeting the threshold set by regulation**); Art. 4 (flat allowance: no children, or children over 21); Art. 5 (no cumulation); Art. 6 (widow caring for a disabled child); Art. 7 (exclusions); Art. 8–11 (payment, prescription); Art. 12 (application) — government bill text, [Chambre des conseillers PDF](https://www.chambredesconseillers.ma/docs/%D9%86%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%B5%20%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B9%D9%8A%D8%A9/%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9%20%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%2058.23.pdf). **[VERIFIED for the bill; the promulgated law is not text-searchable in the ANSS copy — article numbering matches the ANSS/administrative description]**
- **Article 7 exclusions** (unchanged, and the trigger for Loi 41.26): a household does not receive ASD where a spouse or the head of household is (1) a civil servant/agent of the State, territorial collectivities or public establishments; (2) **declared to the private-sector social-security scheme at least once during a period fixed by regulation**; (3) a public- or private-sector pensioner; (4) a beneficiary of non-contributory schemes; and no allowance is paid for a child holding a scholarship larger than the entitlement (difference paid) — bill text, same source. **[VERIFIED for the bill]**
- Consolidated ANSS description of the new mechanism: the exceptional allowance is "d'un montant équivalent à la situation du ménage", granted once for **max 12 months (continuous or not)** from the declaration date, on application by the head of household when the declared employment ends **involuntarily**, and it **re-integrates the household without a waiting period** — [ANSS, Typologies des aides](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/typologies-d-aides). **[VERIFIED]**

### Inferences
- Because Loi 41.26 does not touch Articles 1–4, **nothing in the statutory eligibility architecture changed in 2026**: age limit (<21), the 6-child cap, the 3-child cap on the paternal-orphan complement, the 6-month birth-grant window and the RSU-threshold referral are all still the 2023 text.
- Loi 41.26 is a **targeted anti-poverty-trap fix** (don't force households to choose between declared work and the allowance), not a revaluation.

### Gaps
- I could not read the **promulgated** Arabic text of Loi 58-23 (scanned PDF, no Arabic OCR available), so exact wording of Arts. 1–4/7–12 is quoted from the government bill as tabled, which the National Assembly committee reports indicate was adopted with only drafting amendments. Anyone needing verbatim promulgated wording should read BO 7253 (4 Dec 2023), p. 10223. **NOT CHECKED at primary-source wording level.**
- No amendment of Décret 2.23.1068 (the RSU threshold decree) was found; ANSS lists only the 2023 version.

## Q3. Are the 2025 and 2026 columns of the 2-23-1067 annex legally in force, and from which dates?

### Takeaway
They are **in force** — they are staged yearly columns of the annex as published in December 2023, not discretionary annual decisions — but the **2025 tranche was actually paid from end-November 2025**, not from 1 January 2025, and I found **no dedicated amending decree** for either tranche. Treat "2025" and "2026" as **annex labels**, and "end-Nov 2025 / 2026" as the payment dates.

### Cited Findings
- ANSS presents the figures as **cumulative yearly steps of a single schedule**: "200 DH/enfant : dès 2023 — 250 DH/enfant : à partir de 2025 — 300 DH/enfant : à partir de 2026", with the same triple for non-schooled children (150/175/200), paternal orphans (350/375/400), disability (300/350/400) and widow+disability (450/475/500) — [ANSS, Typologies des aides](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/typologies-d-aides). **[VERIFIED as official rendering]**
- The first step took effect **at the end of November 2025**: "La première revalorisation des aides sociales directes entre en vigueur à la fin du mois de novembre... l'aide mensuelle ... passera de 200 à 250 dirhams par enfant pour les trois premiers enfants... les enfants orphelins de père ... de 350 à 375 dirhams ... les enfants non scolarisés de 6 à 21 ans ... de 150 à 175 dirhams. Une indemnité complémentaire de 100 dirhams reste prévue pour les enfants en situation de handicap. **Le montant minimal que peut percevoir un ménage demeure fixé à 500 dirhams par mois**" — Le Matin, 27 Nov 2025, republished by ANSS: [ANSS press mirror](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/aides-sociales-ce-qui-change-pour-les-familles-des-la-fin-novembre). **[VERIFIED]** Corroborated by [maroc.ma, "La première augmentation entrera en vigueur à compter de la fin du mois de novembre"](https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/aide-sociale-directe-la-premiere-augmentation-entrera-en-vigueur-compter-de-la-fin-du-mois-de-novembre) and [le360](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/aide-sociale-directe-le-chef-du-gouvernement-annonce-une-premiere-augmentation-des-allocations_DAPNTZYPGJE4LFDLEOKFC6VZ2I/). **[VERIFIED]**
- The second step is described by the same official text as "**une deuxième augmentation en 2026**, qui portera la hausse cumulée des prestations entre 50 et 100 dirhams selon les profils" — i.e. 200→300, 150→200, 350→400, 300→400, 450→500 — [ANSS press mirror](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/aides-sociales-ce-qui-change-pour-les-familles-des-la-fin-novembre). **[VERIFIED as intent; the exact 2026 payment month is NOT VERIFIED]**
- **Ranks 4–6 were NOT revalued**: ANSS's table gives a single figure — 36 DH (0–5 and schooled) and 24 DH (not schooled) — with no 2025/2026 columns, and 136 DH (ranks 4–6) / 100 DH (7+) for disability. **[VERIFIED as rendering; INFERRED that these are frozen at 2023 values]**
- Budget framing: the envelope rises to **26.5 bn DH in 2025 and 29 bn DH in 2026** (from 25 bn in 2024) — same ANSS/Le Matin source. **[VERIFIED]**

### Inferences
- Because the annex already contains the 2025 and 2026 columns, **no BO publication date needs to be cited for them to be legally payable in 2026** — a model can treat 300/200/400/500/36/24/136/100 as the 2026 values as of today. The legally citable instrument is the annex of Décret 2.23.1067 as published in BO n° 7253 (4 December 2023), read with the ANSS-published schedule; Décret 2.24.706 (BO 7321) adds the school-return table, not these columns.
- The 500 DH floor continues to apply **on top of** the child schedule in 2026 ("systématiquement revalorisé à 500 DH dans le cas où le total est inférieur à ce seuil") — i.e. a household with one 0–5 child gets 500, not 300.

## Q4. Does the law still condition eligibility on the RSU score ceiling, and is the 500 DH floor still in force?

### Takeaway
**Yes to both, as of 16 November 2026.** The RSU socio-economic index ceiling is still **9.743001**, unchanged since 2023, and the **500 DH monthly floor is still in force through 2026** — it becomes 600 DH only from **1 January 2027** (Décret 2.26.838, approved 12 Nov 2026, publication not yet identified).

### Cited Findings
- Current eligibility conditions, verbatim in French from the ANSS: "Le chef et les membres du ménage ... doivent justifier d'une résidence effective au Maroc ; Ils doivent être inscrits au Registre National de la Population et au Registre Social Unifié ; **L'indice socio-économique de leur famille/ménage, calculé sur le site rsu.ma, doit être inférieur ou égal au seuil d'éligibilité du Régime des ASD fixé à 9,743001**" — [ANSS, Conditions d'éligibilité](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/conditions-d-eligibilite), accessed 16 Nov 2026. **[VERIFIED]**
- The threshold decree is **Décret n° 2.23.1068 of 1 December 2023 "fixant le seuil d'éligibilité au Régime des Aides Sociales Directes"**, still listed as the operative text with no amendment — [ANSS, Lois & décrets](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/lois-decrets). The threshold was announced as 9.743001 to cover ~60% of the population not covered by social security — [TelQuel, 30 Nov 2023](https://mobile.telquel.ma/instant-t/2023/11/30/le-conseil-de-gouvernement-adopte-un-projet-de-decret-fixant-le-seuil-dacces-au-regime-daide-sociale-directe_1843903/). **[VERIFIED]**
- The floor, ANSS wording: "Les ménages ciblés reçoivent un appui financier direct dont le montant est déterminé en fonction de la situation et de la composition de chaque famille, **sans que ce montant ne soit, en aucun cas, inférieur à 500 dirhams**", repeated as "N. B. : Le montant des aides sociales directes mensuelles est systématiquement revalorisé à 500 DH dans le cas où le total est inférieur à ce seuil" — [ANSS, Typologies des aides](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/typologies-d-aides). **[VERIFIED]**
- The **flat allowance** ("aide forfaitaire") is a separate 500 DH: "L'allocation mensuelle dite forfaitaire est fixée à 500 DH... personnes ... âgées de 21 ans et plus, vivant en famille ou en ménage... le même pour toutes les catégories de bénéficiaires... Aucune limite d'âge n'est applicable à cette aide" — same ANSS page. **[VERIFIED]**
- The floor remains 500 in the November 2025 revaluation: "Le montant minimal que peut percevoir un ménage demeure fixé à 500 dirhams par mois" — [ANSS press mirror, 27 Nov 2025](https://anss.gov.ma/fr/aides-sociales-ce-qui-change-pour-les-familles-des-la-fin-novembre). **[VERIFIED]**
- The 600 DH step: Décret 2.26.838 "porte le montant minimal de l'aide de 500 à 600 DH par mois **à compter du 1er janvier 2027**... d'autres augmentations ... pour atteindre 1.000 dirhams à l'horizon 2031" — [Hespress FR, 12 Nov 2026](https://fr.hespress.com/496205-conseil-de-gouvernement-adoption-dun-projet-de-decret-relatif-au-regime-des-aides-sociales-directes.html); [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2026/11/12/aide-sociale-directe-le-gouvernement-acte-par-decret-la-hausse-du-minimum-a-600-dh-des-janvier-2027-1790384/). **[VERIFIED as announced; NOT VERIFIED as published law]**
- Legal-basis note: the 500 DH figure is **not in Loi 58-23** — the statute fixes components, conditions and the referral to a regulation for amounts (Arts. 2, 3, 8), while the figure itself lives in **Décret 2.23.1067** (flat allowance and the annex's revalorisation clause). This is why the 2027 change is being made by decree rather than by statute. **[INFERRED from the bill text + the fact that all three post-2023 amount changes were decrees]**

### Inferences
- For a 2026 OpenFisca parameter set: `rsu_threshold = 9.743001`; `floor_minimum = 500`; `forfait = 500`; child tiers 300/200/36/24; orphan-widow 400/200/36/24; disability 400/136/100; widow+disability 500/136/100; birth grant 2000/1000; school-return 200/300 with a 6-child cap. A 2027 parameter set needs `floor_minimum = 600` **and** `forfait = 600` (same figure in the current design), effective 2027-01-01, and **no change** to child tiers on the evidence available.
- Because the floor is applied *after* summing child amounts, the floor change interacts only with small households (1–2 children or forfait-only households) — consistent with Médias24's statement that the flat-allowance population is ~39% of beneficiaries.
