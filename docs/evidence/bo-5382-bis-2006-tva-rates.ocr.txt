package. This file is machine OCR, not a certified transcription. It exists to
corroborate the machine-readable 2006 text in
`laws/ma_tax_law/data/consolidated/LAR-2006-FR-p1-235.pdf.txt` (pages 95-96),

## What this establishes

The 2006 text numbers the TVA rate articles differently from the 2007+
consolidated editions:

| 2006 (this BO, and LAR 2006) | 2007 and later |
| --- | --- |
| Article 99 - base d'imposition par accord préalable | Article 97 |
| Article 100 - taux normal (20 %) | Article 98 |
| Article 101 - taux réduits (7 % / 10 % / 14 %) | Article 99 |
| Article 102 - taux spécifiques | Article 100 |

So the 2006 reduced-rate list is **Article 101**, and the 2006 specific rates
(100 MAD/hl on wine and alcohol; 4 MAD/g gold and platinum; 0,05 MAD/g silver)
are **Article 102**. Article 100 of the 2007+ editions is the *spécifiques*
article; Article 100 of the 2006 edition is the *normal rate* article. The two
numberings must not be conflated.

The Article 101 item list below matches, item for item, the 38 rows already in
the dataset as `historical_2006_article101`: 14 at 7 %, 16 at 10 %, 8 at 14 %.
